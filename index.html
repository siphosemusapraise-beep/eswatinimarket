Help and safety

Product location

Each listing shows the seller's selected town or area. This demo does not track GPS or verify addresses.

Photos and videos

Choose media from your phone. In this demo it is stored in this browser only; it is not uploaded for other users.

Private chat

Tap “Private chat” on a listing to open a one-to-one WhatsApp conversation with that seller, if they supplied a phone number. Messages are handled by WhatsApp, not stored in EswatiniMarket. Never share your PIN or banking password.

Payments

Choose cash on delivery or cash at collection. Agree on delivery fee, product condition and return terms before paying. Never share your PIN or banking password.

Sign-in and rooms

The sign-in buttons are previews and do not send email codes or authenticate Google yet. Room 1 shows local demo buying, posting and order activity. Room 2 is a local dashboard preview; real user counts, live online status and secure admin-only access require a shared backend with server-side permissions.

Live marketplace requirements

To launch publicly, the app needs accounts, moderation, secure cloud storage for media, a shared database, seller contact and order notifications, privacy controls and backups.
